Juleflet generator geometry, generated 2026-10-20.

Rebuild from the repository root:
  python3 -m pip install -r scripts/generator/requirements.txt
  python3 scripts/generator/build-outlines.py
Use --fonts-only to rebuild only the fonts and their previews.
The generated assets are checked in; deployment does not require Python.

Motifs: 1,525 filled Phosphor icons, Phosphor core 2.1.1.
Source: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core
Retrieved from @iconify-json/ph 1.2.2:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@iconify-json/ph@1.2.2/icons.json
License and author attribution: phosphor-LICENSE.txt.
Only names ending in -fill are included, with the suffix removed.
The catalogue retains the original SVG preview geometry. The individual JSON
files contain flattened, normalised contours derived from those SVGs.
Fifteen simple motifs, among them a nisse, a julebuk and an angel, and the
Danish search aliases are authored for Juleflet.

Additional motifs: 1,088 filled Tabler icons.
Source: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons
Retrieved from @iconify-json/tabler 1.2.42:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@iconify-json/tabler@1.2.42/icons.json
License and author attribution: tabler-LICENSE.txt (MIT).
The filled source bodies are vendored in scripts/generator/vendor/tabler-filled.json.
Use --icons-only to rebuild motifs without rebuilding fonts.

Christmas motifs and animals: 40 icons from eight open sets, chosen for the
figure list. Their SVG bodies are vendored in
scripts/generator/vendor/curated.json, retrieved 2026-10-19 from
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@iconify-json/<set>@<version>/icons.json.
Changes: the outlines are flattened into polygons for cutting, and the
hearts weave them into the paper strips. Motif ids start with the set's prefix.

Fonts: Nunito (800), Bree Serif (400), Oswald (600), Kalam (700), Cinzel (900),
Righteous (400), Josefin Sans (700), Alfa Slab One (400) and Bungee (400), from
the corresponding ofl/<family>/ directories at:
https://github.com/google/fonts
Each family is covered by its own OFL license in fonts/<id>-OFL.txt.
The original source TTF files are in docs/design-start/assets/fonts/.
These are derived, flattened glyph outlines, not redistributed font binaries.
Cinzel and Kalam have slightly thickened outlines where doing so preserves
the number of contours, to leave more paper around fine details.
The sample SVGs show the exact outlines used by the generator.

Construction: independently implemented contour routing; no image solver.
Dense text framing is inspired by Gerth Stolting
Brodal's explanation and slides:
https://cs.au.dk/~gerth/julehjerter/
https://cs.au.dk/~gerth/julehjerter/slides.pdf
No code, glyphs or templates from that site are copied.

Enclosed regions use paired coincident cuts in both sheets, never removed paper.
