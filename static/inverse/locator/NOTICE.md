# Package contents and provenance

The detector, small numerical utilities, browser demo, fixtures and test/report scripts in this addendum were created for this conversation. No third-party implementation, machine-learning model, WASM library or font binary is bundled.

The original photograph and three isolated screenshots were supplied by the user. They are included for reproducible regression experiments, not as a new grant of rights to redistribute or commercially reuse the photographed artwork. The exact originals and their hashes are retained in `inputs/original/` and `inputs/provenance.json`. Prepared sRGB versions are separate test inputs.

Synthetic images were generated by `tools/synthetic.py`. Optional Python/Playwright packages are declared as development dependencies, not bundled. System fonts are used only when rendering the comparison PNGs; their files are not distributed.

This addendum does not contain or supersede the previous inverse-solver packages or the upstream julehjerte repository. It makes no changes to the deployed website.
