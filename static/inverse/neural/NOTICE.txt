Heart paper-body model

Source revision: ac7e1c817ecab7c7dff5ce6b1abba61cd213ff29
See U2NET-LICENSE for the Apache 2.0 license.

Modified for Juleflet: the decoder was adapted using 16 annotated photographs
and synthetic hearts; the encoder was frozen. Exported to ONNX opset 17.
This model estimates paper-body probabilities, not cutting paths or material
strength. The original RGB image and independent geometry checks determine
the templates. Training and evaluation records are in the repository under
docs/inverse/datasets/u2netp-body-adaptation-plan.json and
docs/inverse/datasets/u2netp-balanced-body-results.json.
