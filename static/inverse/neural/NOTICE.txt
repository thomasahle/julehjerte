Heart paper-body model

Based on U2NETP by Xuebin Qin and the U-2-Net contributors.
Upstream: https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
Source revision: ac7e1c817ecab7c7dff5ce6b1abba61cd213ff29
See U2NET-LICENSE for the Apache 2.0 license.

Modified for Juleflet: the decoder was adapted using 16 annotated photographs
and synthetic hearts; the encoder was frozen. Exported to ONNX opset 17.
This model estimates paper-body probabilities, not cutting paths or material
strength. The original RGB image and independent geometry checks determine
the templates. Training and evaluation records are in the repository under
docs/inverse/datasets/u2netp-body-adaptation-plan.json and
docs/inverse/datasets/u2netp-balanced-body-results.json.

Cut-proposal model (cuts-*.onnx, loaded by cuts.js)

A U-Net whose encoder is EfficientNet-B0, initialised from the timm weights
efficientnet_b0.ra_in1k (ImageNet; Apache 2.0, https://huggingface.co/timm/efficientnet_b0.ra_in1k),
and whose decoder was trained from scratch on exact templates of the gallery's
pictures, of the site generator's designs and of generated pictures (warps and
the square's symmetries). Exported to ONNX opset 17, weights in float16. It
proposes where hidden cuts may lie; the exact search and its checks decide the
template. Training records: docs/inverse/HYBRID-NN-SOLVER.md.
